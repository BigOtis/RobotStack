# Robot Future Stack Setup Video Design

## Mood

Practical, capable, approachable. The piece should feel like a clean engineering explainer with enough momentum to sell speed, not like a cloud vendor ad.

## Palette

- Background: `#E7ECEF`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Ink: `#274C77`
- Accent: `#6096BA`
- Support: `#A3CEF1`
- Muted: `#8B8C89`

## Typography

- Display: `Fraunces`
- Body: `IBM Plex Sans`
- Data / labels: `IBM Plex Mono`

## Corners

- Small radius: `6px`
- Panels: `8px`

## Depth

- Flat-to-subtle. Prefer crisp borders, soft shadows, and restrained glows.

## Avoid

- No neon SaaS look
- No dark purple gradients
- No marketing-page hero composition
- No vague claims that are not supported by the skill itself
